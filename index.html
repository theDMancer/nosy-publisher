nosy publisher

The publishing tool behind the nosy channels: short animated films that explain how everyday things work (Tech, Doctor and Builder, in English and Persian).

What it does

  • Uploads finished nosy films to the nosy YouTube channels and schedules when they go public.
  • Sets each video's title, description and thumbnail.
  • Posts the channel's own first comment under its new videos.
  • Reads view and audience statistics of the nosy channels, to plan the next films.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the nosy channels, for those channels. It is not offered to the public, and it never acts on anyone else's account.
