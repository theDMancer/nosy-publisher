Privacy policy

nosy publisher · last updated 25 November 2026

nosy publisher is a private tool used by the owner of the nosy YouTube channels to publish to those channels. It has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for these permissions on the owner's own channels:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload nosy films and set their thumbnails.
  • Manage the YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl): to set titles, descriptions and publish times, and to post and read comments on the channel's own videos.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read views and audience retention of the channel's own videos.

How the data is used

  • Only to publish and manage the nosy channels' own videos and to read their statistics.
  • Comment text and statistics are read to plan replies and future films. They are not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • No data is collected from viewers or from any account other than the nosy channels.

Storage and security

  • The sign-in tokens are kept in private configuration files on the owner's own computer and on the owner's own server, which posts scheduled comments. They are never published or shared.
  • Video files, titles and descriptions are the owner's own work.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with or sold to anyone. The data is only sent to Google's own YouTube APIs to carry out the actions above.

Limited Use

nosy publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is removed, the stored tokens stop working, and they are deleted from the owner's files.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for nosy publisher.
